Identity and materials. The resume is what we rewrite for every Pursue listing (same facts, different order).

The north star is the most important box on this page. If a posting fails it, favorite companies do not save it.

These zero the score (band Out). They are not the checklist. Oscar Health hitting 12/12 and still going Out is this layer, not a scoring bug.
